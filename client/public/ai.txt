## Form Templates
Ready-to-use form templates are available at https://sharaforms.com/templates. Every template has its own dedicated page at https://sharaforms.com/templates/{slug} with a live preview, a detailed description, and an FAQ.

All templates are free to copy, fully customizable, and require no code. Like every SharaForms form, a template supports unlimited submissions, all three presentation modes, built-in calculations, conditional logic, signatures, payments, and dynamic document generation — "see every question, focus on one."

- **Types** (46): https://sharaforms.com/templates/types/{slug}, e.g. order_forms, registration_forms, event_registration_forms, contact_forms, survey_templates, application_forms, quiz_forms, donation_forms, booking_forms, lead_generation_forms.
